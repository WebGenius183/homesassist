Move-In/ End-Of-Tenancy Clean

Whether you’re handing over the keys or just picking them up, we provide deep, detailed cleaning that ensures the property is spotless and ready for its next chapter. This service is ideal for tenants, landlords, letting agents, and housing managers.

✅ What’s Included:

• Full property-wide deep clean (kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms, lounge)
• Inside cabinets, drawers, and wardrobes
• Limescale and grease removal
• Skirting boards, door frames, and switch cleaning
• Interior windows and sills
• Carpet vacuuming and hard floor mopping
• Light fittings and fixtures dusted
• Rubbish removal (optional add-on)

🧰 Additional Services (on request):
• Minor repairs (fixtures, fittings, touch-ups)
• Professional oven and appliance cleaning
• Coordinating tradespeople for last-minute fixes
• Key pick-up/drop-off coordination with estate agents
• Carpet shampoo or steam clean
• Wall scuff removal (non-painted)

🔁 For Letting Agents & Landlords:
• Portfolio maintenance schedules available
• Discounts for multi-property cleans
• Emergency or same-day bookings (subject to availability)
